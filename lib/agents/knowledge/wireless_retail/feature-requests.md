# App Recommendations

## How do I suggest a change to the app?
1. Tap **More** in the bottom nav.
2. Tap **App Recommendations**.
3. Tap **+ New Request**.
4. Fill in:
   - **Title** — short description of what you want changed
   - **Category** — New Feature, Improvement, Bug Fix, UI/UX, Reporting, or Other
   - **Description** — explain what you'd like and why it would help
5. Tap **Submit Request**.

The dev team gets notified immediately and will review your request.

## What happens after I submit?
Your request goes through these stages:
- **Submitted** — received by the dev team
- **Under Review** — being evaluated
- **Planned** — approved and scheduled for development
- **In Progress** — actively being built
- **Completed** — done and live in the app
- **Declined** — not moving forward (reason provided)

You'll get a push notification whenever the status changes.

## Can I see my requests?
Yes. Go to App Recommendations to see all your submissions with their current status. If the dev team added notes, you'll see them when you tap to expand the request.

## Who can submit feature requests?
DMs, Field Leaders, Ops Managers, Owners, and Developers. Employees should talk to their DM about app suggestions.
